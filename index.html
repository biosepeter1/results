📲 Enable Alerts on iPhone

Apple requires web apps to be added to the iPhone Home Screen before notifications can be activated:

  1. Open this portal in Safari (Apple does not support notifications in Chrome for iOS).
  2. Tap the Share button at the bottom of Safari (⎋ or the square with an up-arrow).
  3. Scroll down and tap "Add to Home Screen", then tap Add.
  4. Open Honeyland from your iPhone Home Screen and tap Allow Push Alerts!
💡 Once launched from your Home Screen, you will receive instant vibrations and lock-screen alerts just like a native app.
